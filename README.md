2. What Is NALAI?

NALAI is an AI festival led by TCS AI Club, bringing together Government, GCCs, industry, startups, academia, students and the AI community. The festival will showcase AI solutions, live experiences, talent, innovation and future opportunities.

Keep the website brief. It is not an internal strategy or Government policy document.

Detailed five-pillar strategy essays.

19. Final Design Principle

NALAI should feel like a festival people want to attend — not a document people have to read.

Primary objective: create curiosity → explain the experience quickly → make registration the obvious next action.
